Frequently Asked Questions

Choosing and Managing a SOLYNX System

What types of businesses does SOLYNX work with?

Service businesses and organizations that depend on calls, inquiries, appointments, estimates, reservations, registrations, and consistent customer response.

Do I need to replace my website or CRM?

Not necessarily. SOLYNX reviews the current customer journey and determines what can be connected, improved, retained, or replaced.

Are automation and AI included in every plan?

No. Capabilities depend on the selected plan and approved scope. AI voice and chat are optional capabilities used where they provide practical value.

Can SOLYNX manage the system after setup?

Ongoing management, monitoring, updates, workflow adjustments, and support are available based on the plan and engagement scope.
