03 / What Comes Next

Make the promise repeatable.

These are the near-term decisions that turn a compelling idea into dependable delivery.

01

Agree on scope and economics

Define shoot time, travel, editing, revisions, usage rights, equipment allowance, and delivery costs before setting prices or bundle discounts.

02

Prove the inquiry handoff

Confirm who receives each Digital Media request, who answers it, and how it moves into a quote and production brief.

03

Choose work we can show

Select real, rights-cleared examples; name the source owner and final reviewer before publishing footage or claims.

04

Launch, measure, improve

Track qualified inquiries, booked jobs, response time, delivery hours, revisions, and the real cost of each project. Adjust offers from evidence.
