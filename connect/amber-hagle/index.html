Amber Hagle

Amber Hagle is a Santa Cruz–based filmmaker, photographer, and video editor with an intuitive ability to understand a client's vision and translate it into compelling visual stories.

Over eight and a half years with Women Rocking Business, she created content for entrepreneurs building businesses of their own. Her work also includes projects for Food Revolution Network, Clover, and Project Sanctuary, a nonprofit supporting military and veteran families through therapeutic retreats and ongoing family support.

At SOLYNX, Amber combines creative direction, visual storytelling, and an understanding of what attracts customers to help businesses reach their next stage of growth — from the first conversation through filming and the final edit, identifying what makes each business distinctive and shaping content around its audience and goals.
