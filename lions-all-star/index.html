About the Event

Community. Pride. Tradition.

The Lions All-Star Football Game brings the community together to celebrate student athletes, school pride, local service, and the tradition of North vs South competition.

This milestone event marks the 50th Annual Lions All-Star Football Game and the historic first Girls Flag Football All-Star Game — a landmark moment for student athletes across Lions District 4-A1.
