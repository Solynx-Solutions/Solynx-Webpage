  • Shoot dates. December 2026 through February 28, 2027, subject to availability. December 24–30, 2026 are blackout dates. December 31 and January 1 remain eligible. New promotional bookings require an available eligible date through February 28.

Unpaid dates are not held. Requests inside 72 hours require direct approval and promotional pricing is subject to confirmation. The remaining balance is due on or before the shoot. Editing and delivery begin after payment in full.
  • Travel. Pricing includes up to 60 round-trip miles from Santa Cruz, California. Locations beyond a 30-mile radius require a custom travel quote before booking.
  • Cancellations and no-shows. Client cancellations forfeit the deposit. Cancellations within 72 hours and no-shows are ineligible for rescheduling or credit.
  • Rescheduling. One complimentary reschedule is available with at least 72 hours’ notice, subject to availability within the shoot window and outside blackout dates. A client-requested move past February 28, 2027 incurs a $500 fee, including when no open dates remain before expiry. The fee is due at rescheduling, with at least 72 hours’ notice before the original shoot. Extended shoots must be completed within 60 days and by April 30, 2027. If the fee is declined, the deposit remains non-refundable and the shoot cannot be rescheduled. A second or late request is treated as a cancellation or requires a new booking.
  • Weather, airspace, and emergencies. Unsafe weather or FAA airspace restrictions for drone-dependent shoots qualify for a free reschedule, subject to availability, without using the complimentary reschedule. Crew emergencies are also exempt from the $500 extension fee. If the crew must cancel for an emergency and no mutually agreeable replacement date is available, all amounts paid are refunded. Indoor events proceed rain or shine.
  • Timing. Coverage starts at the agreed time. On-site delays do not extend the end time. Extra coverage is billed at the standard overtime rate in the booking agreement. Standard turnaround counts weekdays excluding federal holidays and begins the next business day after the shoot, once required client assets are received.
  • Exclusions. Non-transferable and cannot be combined with other discounts. Excludes Alpha Flagship, multi-shoot and multi-month bundles, and standalone drone or photo add-ons.
